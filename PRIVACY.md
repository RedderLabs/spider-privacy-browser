# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-04_

Spider Privacy Browser does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login, or identifier of any kind.
- **No telemetry or analytics.** No usage statistics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
- **No third-party backend.** The app does not depend on Google Play Services or any server operated by us — there isn't one.
- **Browsing data stays on the device and is ephemeral.** Tabs and page state live only in memory and are erased when the app closes. The only things stored locally between launches are your **language** and **theme** preference.
- **DNS.** When you enable encrypted DNS, your DNS queries are sent to the resolver you choose (for example Cloudflare, Mullvad, or Quad9). Those providers operate under their own privacy policies. The in-app DoH tunnel runs entirely on your device.
- **Private network (optional).** If you select Tor, traffic is routed by the Orbot app, which you install and control separately.
- **VPN permission.** The app requests Android's VPN permission solely to run a local, on-device DNS-over-HTTPS tunnel. It does **not** route your traffic to us.

Because there is no server and no data collection, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose.
